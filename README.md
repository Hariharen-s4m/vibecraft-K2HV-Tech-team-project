🌐 About ClassSync AI
ClassSync AI is a web-based smart attendance management system created by K2HV** for ClassSync AI 2026**.
The idea behind ClassSync AI is simple:
Attendance should not just tell students where they are today — it should help them understand where they can be in the future.
Instead of only displaying an attendance percentage, ClassSync AI provides tools that allow students to analyze their attendance, plan ahead, simulate different situations, and make informed decisions before their attendance becomes critical.
This project is also our team's first hackathon project, giving us an opportunity to combine problem-solving, web development, data handling, UI/UX design, and teamwork into one complete application.
 
🎯 Problem Statement
Attendance management can become difficult for students when they have multiple subjects, different timetables, upcoming holidays, ODs, medical leave, and minimum-attendance requirements.
A student may know their current attendance percentage but still not know:
•	How many classes they can miss.
•	How many classes they need to attend.
•	Whether they can recover from low attendance.
•	What their attendance could look like on a future date.
•	How an OD or medical leave could affect their attendance.
•	When reaching the required attendance percentage becomes mathematically impossible.
ClassSync AI aims to bring these calculations and planning tools together in a single, easy-to-use platform.
 
💡 Our Solution
ClassSync AI combines:
Timetable Data → Attendance Calculations → Future Planning → Simulations → Analytics → AI Assistance
The application uses timetable information and attendance data to help students understand their current position and explore possible future scenarios.
 
✨ Key Features
📊 Attendance Calculator
Calculate and analyze attendance using the student's current attendance information and selected planning date.
The system is designed to help answer questions such as:
•	What is my current attendance?
•	How many classes remain?
•	How many classes do I need to attend?
•	Can I reach the required attendance percentage?
 
📅 Multiple Class Sections
ClassSync AI incorporates timetable information for multiple class sections.
Students can select their relevant section so that attendance planning can be based on the appropriate timetable.
 
🎯 Future Attendance Planning
Instead of looking only at the current attendance percentage, ClassSync AI allows students to select a future planning date.
This makes it possible to analyze attendance over a specific period rather than treating attendance as a static number.
 
🚨 Irreversible Detention Warning
One of the important concepts in ClassSync AI is identifying when attendance can no longer be recovered to the required level within the selected period.
When recovery becomes mathematically impossible, the system can display an:
IRREVERSIBLE DETENTION
warning.
This helps students identify critical attendance situations early.
 
📈 Attendance Analytics
Attendance information can be represented visually through charts and analytics.
Visualizing attendance makes it easier to understand the student's current situation at a glance.
 
📝 OD Simulator
The OD simulator allows students to explore how an OD scenario could affect their attendance.
Instead of making assumptions about the outcome, students can use the simulator to understand the possible effect of planned attendance-related situations.
 
🏥 Medical Leave Simulator
The medical-leave simulator provides another scenario-planning tool for students who need to understand how medical leave could affect their attendance.
 
🤖 AI Attendance Advisor
ClassSync AI also includes an AI-based attendance advisor interface.
The advisor is designed to help students understand their attendance situation and provide guidance based on the information available in the application.

"Where is my attendance going?"
